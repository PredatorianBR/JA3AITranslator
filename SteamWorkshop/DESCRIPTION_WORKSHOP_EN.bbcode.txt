[h2]Quick Setup (30 Seconds)[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Subscribe and [b]enable JA3 AI Translator[/b] in the Jagged Alliance 3 Mod Manager.

[*]Select your [b]AI Provider[/b] and paste your [b]API Key[/b] (e.g., a free Google AI Studio Gemini key, or pick Ollama / LM Studio for local offline translation) and choose your [b]Target Language[/b] (defaults to [i]Auto - Game Language[/i]).
